# APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & HIGHER SCIENCES
## RESIDENTIAL LIFE & HOSTEL ADMINISTRATION HANDBOOK
**Edition:** 2025–2026 Academic Year  
**Office of the Dean of Student Affairs & Chief Warden**

### CHAPTER 1: RESIDENTIAL PHILOSOPHY & ADMISSION TO HALLS OF RESIDENCE

#### 1.1 Objective and Residential Community Standards
The residential halls at Apex Institute of Technology & Higher Sciences are envisioned as vibrant learning communities promoting scholarship, camaraderie, civic responsibility, and mutual respect. Living on campus is a privilege extended to enrolled students subject to strict adherence to institutional standards and codes of conduct.

#### 1.2 Room Allocation and Possession Protocols
1. Rooms in residential halls are allocated annually by the Chief Warden’s Office based on seniority, academic standing (CGPA), and program requirements.
2. First-year and second-year undergraduate students are mandated to reside in shared double-occupancy rooms in Junior Residential Quads (Blocks A through D).
3. Single-occupancy rooms in Senior Towers (Blocks E, F, and G) are reserved exclusively for third-year, fourth-year, and postgraduate students maintaining a minimum CGPA of 7.50 without active disciplinary reprimands.
4. Room allotment is non-transferable. Subletting, unauthorized swapping of rooms, or sheltering unregistered individuals is an offense resulting in immediate cancellation of hostel allotment and forfeiture of the caution deposit.
5. Inmates must complete a physical inventory check-sheet at the time of taking possession, cataloging the condition of furniture, electrical fittings, window screens, and study carrels.

### CHAPTER 2: RESIDENTIAL CODE OF CONDUCT & COMMUNITY DISCIPLINE

#### 2.1 General Decorum and Civility
All residents must treat fellow residents, administrative wardens, security personnel, mess workers, and maintenance staff with dignity and respect. Verbal abuse, harassment, intimidation, or disruptive behavior will attract severe disciplinary action under the Standing Student Disciplinary Ordinance.

#### 2.2 Anti-Ragging Statutory Declaration
1. Ragging in any shape, manner, or form—whether physical, psychological, verbal, electronic, or communicative—is strictly prohibited by Supreme Court mandates and Institute regulations.
2. Any resident found engaging in, abetting, or failing to report incidents of ragging shall face immediate suspension, summary eviction from the hostel, filing of a First Information Report (FIR) with the state police authorities, and permanent expulsion from the Institute.

#### 2.3 Prohibited Substances and Contraband
1. The entire residential campus is an absolute Zero-Tolerance Zone for alcoholic beverages, tobacco products, vaping devices, narcotics, psychotropic substances, and flammable fireworks.
2. Possession, consumption, or storage of liquor, illicit drugs, or drug paraphernalia inside hostel rooms or residential common areas shall lead to immediate expulsion and criminal prosecution.

#### 2.4 Electrical Safety and Prohibited Appliances
1. To prevent electrical overloads and fire hazards, students are strictly prohibited from using high-wattage electrical heating appliances inside hostel rooms. This includes electric immersion heaters, hotplates, induction cooktops, room heaters, electric kettles exceeding 500W, and toaster ovens.
2. Permitted electrical devices are limited to laptop chargers, mobile phone chargers, table lamps, and personal hair dryers (under 1000W).
3. Unauthorized appliances will be confiscated on discovery, and an administrative fine of Rs. 3,000 shall be levied per confiscated item.

### CHAPTER 3: CAMPUS INGRESS, EGRESS, AND NIGHT CURFEW REGULATIONS

#### 3.1 Mandatory Campus Ingress & Night Curfew [STRICT RESIDENTIAL LOCKDOWN POLICY]
All campus entrance gates, library halls, and academic departmental complexes shall be locked strictly at 22:00 hours (10:00 PM). All resident students must be physically inside their assigned hostel blocks by 22:00 hours. No student is permitted outside their hostel block under any circumstance between 22:00 hours and 06:00 hours. Security supervisors at hostel entrances are mandated to log biometric attendance and physically bolt the external iron security gates promptly at 22:00 hours. Any resident student arriving at the hostel gate after 22:00 hours shall have their name entered into the Late Entry Violation Register, incur an automatic fine of Rs. 1,000 for the first offense, and be referred to the Chief Warden. There are absolutely no exceptions to this curfew, regardless of departmental projects, study groups, or extracurricular activities.

#### 3.2 Night Outstation Pass Protocol
1. Any resident requiring to travel out of town or stay overnight away from the campus must submit a formal Night Outstation Pass via the Student ERP Portal at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance.
2. The Outstation Pass must be authenticated by written parental consent dispatched from the registered parental telephone number or email address on file.
3. Returning students must punch their biometric clearance at the main gate and deposit their signed slip with the resident block warden.

#### 3.3 Emergency Medical Exemption
In instances of acute medical distress occurring during curfew hours (22:00 to 06:00), the resident on-duty warden must be immediately contacted. The warden will arrange for the campus emergency ambulance to convey the patient to the University Medical Center. Under no circumstances should individual students venture unaccompanied past the hostel gates during curfew.

### CHAPTER 4: GUEST AND VISITOR REGULATIONS

#### 4.1 Permitted Visiting Hours
1. Parents and authorized immediate guardians registered during matriculation may visit residents in the designated Hostel Visitor Lounges between 09:00 hours and 18:00 hours on Saturdays, Sundays, and official institute holidays.
2. Under no circumstances are visitors, parents, or guardians permitted entry into the private corridors or student dorm rooms of residential hostels.
3. Visitors of the opposite gender are strictly restricted to the Ground Floor Administrative Reception and Visitors' Lounge and must depart prior to 18:00 hours.

#### 4.2 Guest House Accommodation
Visiting parents desiring overnight accommodation on campus may reserve rooms in the Institute Executive Guest House via the Estate Management portal, subject to room availability, payment of standard tariffs (Rs. 2,000 per room per night), and advance booking of at least seven (7) days.

### CHAPTER 5: DINING HALL AND MESS ADMINISTRATION

#### 5.1 Meal Timings and Meal Card Protocols
The Central Student Dining Hall operates under the following strict schedule seven days a week:
- Breakfast: 07:30 hours to 09:30 hours
- Lunch: 12:00 hours to 14:15 hours
- Evening High Tea & Snacks: 17:00 hours to 18:15 hours
- Dinner: 19:30 hours to 21:30 hours

Dining entry is authenticated through the electronic RFID Student Smart ID Card. Meal entitlements are strictly personal and non-transferable.

#### 5.2 Mess Hygiene and Food Delivery Policies
1. Outside commercial food delivery agents (Swiggy, Zomato, etc.) are strictly prohibited from driving onto the residential hostel lanes. All food parcels must be collected by the ordering student at the Main Campus Gate Security Booth prior to the 22:00 hours curfew.
2. Cooking inside individual hostel rooms is strictly prohibited. Any resident found cooking meals, boiling eggs, or operating hotplates in rooms will be penalized under Chapter 2.4.
3. Taking dining hall crockery, steel plates, glasses, or cutlery out of the central dining hall premises into student hostel rooms is deemed theft and will incur a community service penalty.

---

### CHAPTER 6: MAINTENANCE, DAMAGE LIABILITY, AND CAUTION DEPOSITS

#### 6.1 Routine Room Maintenance
1. Housekeeping personnel clean common areas, corridors, washrooms, and stairwells daily between 08:30 hours and 14:00 hours.
2. Cleaning of private dorm rooms is the personal responsibility of the individual residents. Rooms must be kept hygienic, orderly, and free of waste accumulations.
3. Maintenance requests for plumbing, carpentry, or electrical repairs must be submitted electronically through the Campus Facilities Helpdesk ticket system.

#### 6.2 Property Damage and Group Liability
1. If institutional furniture, fixtures, ceiling fans, glass windowpanes, or electronic infrastructure in a hostel room suffer damage due to negligence or malice, the repairing cost plus a 25% administrative levy will be deducted from the occupants' Hostel Caution Deposit.
2. In cases where property damage occurs in shared common areas (such as television lounges, elevators, water cooler stations, or corridors) and the specific perpetrator cannot be identified, collective group liability shall be levied equally across all residents of that floor or residential wing.

### CHAPTER 7: QUIET HOURS, LEISURE AMENITIES, AND SPORTS FACILITIES

#### 7.1 Mandatory Quiet Hours
1. To ensure an environment conducive to intellectual focus and restful sleep, Mandatory Quiet Hours are enforced in all residential hostels between 23:00 hours and 06:00 hours daily.
2. During Quiet Hours:
   - Volume on audio devices, speakers, or musical instruments must not be audible beyond the physical confines of the room.
   - Gatherings, parties, shouting, or loud discussions in corridors and common stairways are strictly prohibited.
   - Use of hostel Common Television Rooms is suspended after 23:00 hours.

#### 7.2 Recreational Facilities
Common recreation rooms equipped with table tennis boards, foosball tables, and chess sets are open daily from 06:00 hours to 21:45 hours. All sports gear must be returned to the caretaker desk before the 22:00 hours curfew lockdown.

### CHAPTER 8: WARDEN ADVISORY COMMITTEE & DISCIPLINARY SANCTIONS

#### 8.1 Disciplinary Sanction Scale
Infractions of this handbook shall be adjudicated by the Hostel Disciplinary Committee (HDC), presided over by the Chief Warden. Sanctions range in severity:
- **Level 1 (Minor Violation):** Written warning notice and mandatory parent notification.
- **Level 2 (Moderate Violation):** Administrative monetary fine ranging from Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 5,000, along with mandatory community service (e.g., library catalog assistance).
- **Level 3 (Severe Violation):** Suspension of hostel residency for one semester, forfeiture of hostel fees, and formal notation on the student's conduct certificate.
- **Level 4 (Critical / Criminal Breach):** Permanent expulsion from all campus housing, academic suspension, and referral to law enforcement.

#### 8.2 Appeals Procedure
A student penalized by the HDC may lodge a formal appeal to the Dean of Student Affairs within five (5) business days of the sanction notice. The decision of the Dean of Student Affairs shall be final and binding.

### CHAPTER 9: HOSTEL SUSTAINABILITY, RECYCLING & WATER CONSERVATION

#### 9.1 Environmental Responsibility & Conservation Protocols
Apex Institute of Technology is committed to maintaining a net-zero, environmentally sustainable residential campus. Residents are required to actively participate in energy and water conservation initiatives:
1. All residents must switch off lights, ceiling fans, computer monitors, and exhaust fans whenever exiting dorm rooms or common study chambers. Defaulting rooms identified during random security sweeps will be subject to a collective green audit levy of Rs. 500 per incident.
2. Water waste through dripping taps or leaking cisterns must be reported within two hours to the plumbing helpdesk. The washing of personal vehicles, motorcycles, or bicycles within residential courtyards using hostel hosepipes is strictly forbidden.
3. Waste segregation at source is mandatory. Residents must utilize designated color-coded collection bins: Green for organic food waste, Blue for dry paper and cardboard, and Red for electronic waste or broken metal parts.

#### 9.2 Solar Water Heating Infrastructure
Hostel blocks are equipped with centralized rooftop solar water heating collectors. Solar hot water supply is scheduled daily from 06:00 hours to 09:30 hours and 18:00 hours to 20:30 hours during winter months. Tampering with roof plumbing valves or safety relief fixtures constitutes severe physical vandalism under Chapter 6.

### CHAPTER 10: RESIDENT WELFARE COUNCIL & GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

#### 10.1 Composition of the Residential Hall Council
Each residential hostel block maintains an elected Student Welfare Council comprising:
- Block General Secretary (senior resident)
- Dining & Mess Committee Convener
- Sports & Cultural Representative
- Health, Sanitation, and Green Audit Secretary
The Council meets bi-weekly with the Resident Warden to evaluate mess meal quality, review facilities maintenance tickets, coordinate intra-hostel tournaments, and propose community improvements.

#### 10.2 Formal Complaint Filing and Escalation Timeline
1. Routine maintenance complaints must be resolved within forty-eight (48) hours of electronic ticket logging.
2. In the event of unresolved grievances, residents may escalate issues sequentially: First to the Resident Warden, then to the Chief Warden, and ultimately to the Dean of Student Affairs.
3. Anonymous, frivolous, or retaliatory complaints directed against fellow students or administrative wardens without substantiating evidence are strictly prohibited.
